# Video: Impulskäufe und die 24-Stunden-Regel

**Kanal:** Brokey (Finanzen humorvoll erklärt)
**Länge:** ca. 40-48 Sekunden
**Format:** 9:16, Papier-Look, Stop-Motion-Feel
**Status:** Entwurf, Zahlen und Aussagen vor dem Upload prüfen (siehe Faktencheck)

## 1. Eckdaten

- **Thema:** Spontane Käufe, die man nicht braucht
- **Lerneffekt (genau einer):** Wer einen unnötigen Kauf einen Tag liegen lässt, spart oft Geld, weil der Impuls meist verfliegt.
- **Zielgruppe:** breit, besonders Teenager, Azubis, Berufseinsteiger
- **Ton:** trockener Erzähler, sarkastisch. Dem Stickman passiert das Chaos.
- **Hook:** Verlustangst und Selbsterkenntnis ("Du kaufst gleich etwas, das du nicht brauchst.")
- **Running Gag:** Eine goldene Münze fliegt im Hintergrund davon (in jeder Szene sichtbar).
- **Hinweis Monetarisierung:** Das Video ist unter einer Minute. Für das Creator Rewards Program gelten laut früherer Planung Videos über einer Minute, aktuelle Bedingungen in der App prüfen. Dieses Video eignet sich gut als Start- und Testvideo.

## 2. Sprechertext (ca. 107 Wörter)

| Zeit | Szene | Sprechertext |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 s | Hook | "Du kaufst gleich etwas, das du nicht brauchst. Und du weißt es noch nicht." |
| 4-13 s | Setup | "Brokey will nur kurz gucken. Dann blinkt es: Nur heute, fast ausverkauft. Und plötzlich braucht er unbedingt ein Gerät, das Bananen schneidet." |
| 13-23 s | Chaos | "Paket da, Freude kurz, ab in den Schrank. Zu den fünf anderen Dingen, die er auch unbedingt brauchte." |
| 23-37 s | Lerneffekt | "Der Trick: die 24-Stunden-Regel. Pack es in den Warenkorb und warte einen Tag. Ist der Wunsch dann noch da, kauf bewusst. Ist er weg, hast du Geld gespart." |
| 37-43 s | Punchline | "Und ja, du hast gerade an deinen Warenkorb gedacht." |
| 43-47 s | Outro | "Folg mir für mehr Geld-Chaos, das du wirklich verstehst. Keine Finanzberatung." |

Sprechtempo: ruhig, trocken, leichte Pausen vor den Pointen ("Nur heute, fast ausverkauft." / "...die er auch unbedingt brauchte.").

## 4. Prompts für Bilder

Setze vor jeden Szenen-Prompt die drei Basis-Blöcke.

### Basis-Blöcke (immer vorne einsetzen)

**CHARACTER**
```
Brokey: a stick figure with a big round white head, thin black marker limbs, a red knit beanie with a fluffy pom-pom, heavy half-closed tired eyes with dark eye bags, a small flat mouth. Always exactly the same design as the reference image.
```

**STYLE**
```
Hand-drawn black marker doodle on a white sheet of paper that fills the entire frame edge to edge, visible paper fibers, fine grain, faint creases, slightly wobbly marker lines, flat red color on the beanie, small flat color accents only where needed (gold for coins, brown for wallet), no table, no paper edges, no hands, aspect ratio 9:16.
```

**AVOID**
```
Avoid: text, letters, numbers, watermark, logos, brand names, extra characters, hands, realistic style, 3D render, gradients, photo background.
```

### Szene 1: Hook
```
Brokey freezes in front of a giant doodled shopping cart with a mischievous grin, eyes bulging, mouth wide open, sweat drops flying, a gold coin flying away in the background, black motion lines.
```

### Szene 2a: Nachts scrollen
```
Brokey sits hunched in front of a glowing laptop at night, a small crescent moon doodled in the corner, a tiny doodled devil with horns on one shoulder whispering and a tiny angel with a halo facepalming on the other, Brokey with a smug unaware grin, a gold coin flying away in the background.
```

### Szene 2b: "Nur heute"
```
Brokey stares hopefully at a huge doodled starburst-shaped sign with percent-like marks (no letters), next to it a ridiculous single-purpose banana-slicing gadget sparkling, Brokey's eyes shining, a gold coin flying away in the background.
```

### Szene 3a: Paket
```
A doodled delivery box lands on the paper with a thud, Brokey opens it with a short joyful smile and holds up the shiny banana-slicing gadget, tiny confetti dots around him, a gold coin flying away in the background.
```

### Szene 3b: Schrank
```
Brokey stands in front of a doodled wardrobe overflowing with dusty useless gadgets with cobwebs, one gadget has a sad face, Brokey stares at the viewer resigned and deadpan, the new banana gadget in his hand, a gold coin flying away in the background.
```

### Szene 4a: Warenkorb und Uhr
```
Brokey places the banana gadget into a small doodled shopping cart with a big doodled ticking clock beside it, Brokey sits down patiently with his arms crossed, bored deadpan expression, a gold coin flying away in the background.
```

### Szene 4b: Wunsch verpufft
```
A small cartoon craving cloud with a tiny sparkly "want" face above the shopping cart slowly evaporates into smoke, Brokey with a sudden realization expression and a lightbulb above his head, his wallet still full next to him, a gold coin flying away in the background.
```

### Szene 5: Punchline
```
Brokey looks straight at the viewer with a dead-eyed resigned stare, behind him a tiny doodled shopping cart with a smug grin peeks out from the edge, a gold coin flying away in the background.
```

### Szene 6: Outro
```
Brokey waves tiredly toward the viewer, one gold coin falls at his feet and rolls off, relaxed slumped pose.
```

## 5. Prompts für Videos (je 6 Sekunden)

Starte jeden Clip mit dem passenden Standbild als Startbild.

**VIDEO-STYLE (an jeden Prompt hängen)**
```
Stop-motion feel at about 10 frames per second, hand-drawn line boil effect, the paper background stays perfectly static and fills the entire frame, camera locked flat top-down, no hands, no tools. No speech, no music, only subtle cartoon sound effects.
```

**Clip 1 (Hook, nutze die ersten 4 s)**
```
0-2s: the grinning shopping cart slowly rolls toward Brokey, he freezes. 2-4s: his eyes bulge and he starts shaking, sweat drops fly. 4-6s: he slowly lifts his hands in front of his face. [VIDEO-STYLE]
```

**Clip 2a**
```
0-2s: Brokey scrolls on the laptop, the devil on his shoulder whispers. 2-4s: the angel facepalms and slides down. 4-6s: Brokey nods along with a smug grin. [VIDEO-STYLE]
```

**Clip 2b**
```
0-2s: the starburst sign starts blinking, Brokey's eyes shine. 2-4s: the banana gadget sparkles and hops closer. 4-6s: Brokey reaches for it, mouth open in awe. [VIDEO-STYLE]
```

**Clip 3a**
```
0-2s: the box thuds onto the paper. 2-4s: Brokey rips it open and holds up the gadget with a joyful smile. 4-6s: his smile fades, he looks bored at the gadget. [VIDEO-STYLE]
```

**Clip 3b**
```
0-2s: Brokey opens the wardrobe, dusty gadgets tumble out. 2-4s: he looks at them, then at the new gadget. 4-6s: he places it in the wardrobe and closes the door with a deadpan stare at the viewer. [VIDEO-STYLE]
```

**Clip 4a**
```
0-2s: Brokey drops the gadget in the cart, the clock starts ticking. 2-4s: Brokey sits down and crosses his arms, the clock hands spin fast. 4-6s: a tiny calendar page flips, Brokey yawns. [VIDEO-STYLE]
```

**Clip 4b**
```
0-2s: the craving cloud above the cart flickers. 2-4s: it shrinks and evaporates with a little puff of smoke. 4-6s: Brokey's face lights up with realization, the wallet bulges with coins and a lightbulb pings above his head. [VIDEO-STYLE]
```

**Clip 5 (Punchline)**
```
0-2s: Brokey stares at the viewer, deadpan. 2-4s: the tiny smug shopping cart slides into view behind him. 4-6s: Brokey slowly turns his head, narrows his eyes at it, the cart hides. [VIDEO-STYLE]
```

**Clip 6 (Outro, nutze 4 s)**
```
0-2s: Brokey waves tiredly toward the viewer. 2-4s: a gold coin drops at his feet and rolls out of frame. 4-6s: he sighs and shrugs. [VIDEO-STYLE]
```
